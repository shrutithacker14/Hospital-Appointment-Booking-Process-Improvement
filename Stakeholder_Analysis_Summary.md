# Stakeholder Analysis & RACI Governance Matrix — Zydus Hospitals Case Study

> **Portfolio Governance Note:** This stakeholder analysis models role-based healthcare personas for a hypothetical **Zydus Hospitals** case study. It serves as an interview-ready starting framework to validate with project sponsors during discovery.

## 1. Power-Interest Grid (Mendelow's Matrix)
* **Manage Closely (High Power, High Interest):** Zydus Hospitals COO / Medical Director (`SH-05`), Zydus OPD Consulting Doctors & Visiting Specialists (`SH-03`), Lead Business Analyst (`Shruti Thacker`).
* **Keep Satisfied (High Power, Low/Medium Interest):** Zydus Hospital IT & HIS Architecture Team (`SH-06`), Data Privacy & ABDM Compliance Officer (`SH-08`).
* **Keep Informed (Low/Medium Power, High Interest):** Patients & Family Caregivers (`SH-01`), Zydus Front-Desk Receptionists & OPD Billing Staff (`SH-02`), OPD Floor Nursing & Queue Coordinators (`SH-04`).
* **Monitor (Low/Medium Power, Low/Medium Interest):** Finance & Revenue Reconciliation Team (`SH-07`), Third-Party WhatsApp/SMS & Payment Gateway Vendors.
